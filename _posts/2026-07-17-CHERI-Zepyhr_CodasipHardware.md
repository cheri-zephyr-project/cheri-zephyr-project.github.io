We are pleased to announce that CHERI-Zephyr was successfully demonstrated on CHERI-certified hardware - the Codasip X730, 64-bit CHERI-RISC-V platform, and the Codasip v739, 32-bit CHERI-RISC-V platform.

"This achievement wouldn't have been possible without dedicated collaboration between our academics and the wider CHERI-Alliance community including Codasip's hardware and software engineers who developed the CHERI-RISC-V platform." says Jennifer Jackson, the lead on the project.

This is another important step forward in bringing CHERI-Zephyr closer to real-world deployment on memory safe systems.
